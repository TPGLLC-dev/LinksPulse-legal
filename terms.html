2. The App Is Not a Safety Service

This is the most important term in this document. LinksPulse is a round-planning convenience. It is not a lightning detection or warning system, a weather-alert service, or an emergency information service, and it must never be used as a substitute for any of them.

  • Lightning is the danger that matters, and the App cannot see it coming. A golf course is one of the most exposed places to be in a thunderstorm, and Central Florida has more lightning than almost anywhere in the United States. A good weather score for your round is a forecast, not a promise. If you hear thunder, stop play and get to proper shelter, whatever the App shows.
  • Follow the course over the App, always. A course's lightning siren or horn, its starters, marshals and staff, and any suspension of play govern. If the App and the course disagree, the course is right.
  • Alerts may be missing, late, or wrong. The App displays National Weather Service alerts verbatim, but it depends on that service being reachable and on your device having a network connection. An absence of alerts in the App is not a statement that conditions are safe.
  • Heat is a real risk. Summer heat and humidity on an open course can cause heat exhaustion and heat stroke. A heat note in the App is general information, not medical advice. Drink water, use shade and a cart, and stop if you feel unwell.
  • Florida courses have wildlife, including alligators and snakes near water. Keep your distance, do not retrieve balls from water or thick vegetation, and follow the course's guidance.
  • In an emergency, call 911. Do not use the App.

Golf, and driving a golf cart, carry a risk of injury. You assume that risk. Judge conditions yourself when you arrive, and do not play because an app scored the day well.

6. Data Accuracy and How Recommendations Are Made

The App combines third-party live data with a fixed table of information about each course, and produces a ranking. Everything it shows is provided for planning purposes only and may be inaccurate, incomplete or out of date.

  • Live readings come from third parties. Weather forecasts from Open-Meteo; weather alerts from the National Weather Service; drive times from TomTom. We do not produce this data, cannot verify it, and are not responsible for its accuracy or availability.
  • Forecasts are forecasts. Conditions shown for your round are predictions and will sometimes be wrong. The further ahead the day, the less the hour-by-hour forecast can be trusted, and the App scores it that way.
  • A recommendation is only as good as what it can see. The App does not know which tee times are open, and often does not know the current green fee. When courses are too close to call on what it does know, it says so rather than naming a winner.
  • Green fees, cart policy, walking rules, dress codes, hours, maintenance closures and who may play change without notice. Some courses are open only to members or to guests of a particular resort. This information is recorded from public sources, is not continuously verified, and may be wrong. Confirm anything that matters with the course before you rely on it. The App marks facts it has not confirmed; treat those with particular care.
  • Drive times are estimates. They come from TomTom, using live traffic for today and usual traffic for a later day. A real drive can take longer, and the App cannot anticipate crashes, closures or events.
  • Pictures may not show the course as it is today. Aerial images come from periodic government surveys and can be several years old.
