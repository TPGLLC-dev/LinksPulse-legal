Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I book a tee time in the App? No. LinksPulse recommends a course and sends you to that course's own booking page. It doesn't show live tee-time availability yet.
  • Why does a course say to confirm the green fee or rules? Fees, cart and walking rules, dress codes and hours change without notice. The App marks anything it hasn't confirmed, so check with the course before you rely on it.
  • Why can't I see a certain course? Some courses, such as Bay Hill and Reunion, only take their own resort's guests. LinksPulse hides them unless you say you're staying there.
  • Do I have to share my location? No. You can pick a starting area or type a hotel or address instead. If you do share it, it's rounded to about 100 meters and used only to work out drive times. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Drive times look off. Today's drive times use live traffic, and later days use usual traffic for the time you'd leave. Check that you have a network connection and that your starting point is right.
  • I bought Pro but it's not unlocked. Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, so use the same one you bought it with.
  • How do I get a refund? Apple processes all purchases. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • How do I delete my data? LinksPulse has no accounts and keeps nothing about you on our side. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on your phone.
