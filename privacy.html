LinksPulse — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026  •  Last Updated: October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Penelope Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in LinksPulse ("the App"). LinksPulse recommends which Central Florida golf course to play, given where you are starting from, when you want to play, and what matters to you.

The short version. LinksPulse has no accounts. We do not know who you are, and we keep no record of your use of the App. To show real drive times, the App sends your starting point, rounded to about 100 meters and with nothing that identifies you, to our routing service, which asks TomTom for the drive times and keeps that rounded point for at most an hour. To show Pro's price and whether Pro is unlocked, the App checks with our purchase provider using a random, anonymous ID it creates on your phone, along with your device's app identifier. If you buy Pro, Apple handles the payment and that anonymous ID is recorded as having bought it; none of this is linked to your name or email.

1. Information We Collect

We collect nothing about who you are. We operate no account system and no database. The one service we run, for drive times, receives a rounded starting point and nothing else about you (Section 3). There is no sign-up, no email address, no username and no password. We have no way to contact you and no profile of you to build, sell or lose. The one exception is a purchase record: so the App can show and recognize Pro, our purchase provider receives an anonymous ID and your device's app identifier (Section 3).

The App does handle a small amount of information locally on your device, described below. What leaves your device is a rounded starting point for drive times and, if you make one, a record of a Pro purchase (Section 3).

2. Location

Location is optional. The App works fully without it: instead of your device's position you can choose a starting area from a list (for example, the Disney area), and every feature behaves the same way.

If you do choose to start from your current location:

  • Foreground only. The App requests "While Using the App" permission. It does not request, and cannot obtain, background location.
  • One reading, on request. A single position is read at the moment you choose "My location." The App does not follow your position, does not use geofencing, and does not run when it is closed.
  • Rounded before it leaves. To work out drive times, the position is rounded to about 100 meters and sent to our routing service, which asks TomTom for the routes. It is sent with nothing that identifies you or your device, the service keeps the rounded point for at most an hour (so the next search from the same spot is quick), and no record of where you have been is kept, on the device or anywhere else.

Typing a starting place (a hotel, street address or town) is the other way to set where you start. What you type is sent to Apple's map search, which returns its location. If Apple's search is unavailable or finds nothing, the text goes to our routing service instead, which looks it up with TomTom. The place it finds is then used exactly like a current location, above. The App keeps the place it found (its name and position) on your phone as part of your saved plan, below, so it does not have to look it up again; the text you typed is not kept.

The same applies when you pick one of the preset starting areas, although those are fixed points built into the App and say nothing about you.

Only the routing service ever receives anything about you, and only a rounded starting point. The weather and alert services are asked about the courses, never about you.

A few actions hand you over to someone else, and only when you take them:

  • The Map tab displays Apple's map. Apple serves the map for the area on screen, under Apple's privacy policy. The App does not show your own position on that map.
  • Directions hand the course's location to Apple Maps.
  • Booking and course links open the course's own website or its booking provider (for example, a course's tee-time page) in a browser window inside the App, provided by Apple (Safari View Controller). That window is Safari's, not ours: the App cannot see the pages you visit there, what you type, or whether you booked. We do not book tee times and receive nothing back. When you close it, the App asks whether you got a tee time, and saves only what you choose to enter (see section 4). Anything you enter there, including payment details, goes to that site under its terms and privacy policy, not ours.
  • Emailing support opens your own mail app. If you send the email, we receive what you choose to write, and use it only to answer you.

4. What Is Stored on Your Device

The App saves a small amount of data in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your appearance setting (light, dark or system).
  • A flag recording that you have seen the introduction, so it is not shown twice.
  • Tee times you choose to save: the course, day, time and number of players you enter after booking. They are removed after the day of the round, or when you tap Remove.
  • Reminders for those tee times, if you allow notifications: one the evening before and one two hours before. They are scheduled by your phone's own notification system, contain only the course, time and number of players, and are never sent from or through a server. We have no push notification service and no device token. You can turn them off in your phone's Settings at any time.
  • If you tap Share on a saved tee time, the App hands its course, day, time, number of players and a map link to your phone's own share screen or its New Event calendar screen. You choose where it goes and whether to save it; the App never sends it anywhere itself and never reads your calendar.
  • The anonymous purchase ID and a cached copy of whether Pro is unlocked, kept by our purchase provider's library.
  • Your plan, so it is ready the next time you open the App: where you are staying (one of the App's areas, a place you typed, or simply that you chose "My location"), number of players, 18 or 9 holes, budget, the things you said you'd like, and the golf resort you are staying at, if any. The day and the tee time are never kept. When you chose "My location", your position is not stored; the App asks your phone for it again when it opens, and only if you have already allowed location. You can erase the saved plan at any time with "Clear my saved plan" in Settings.

None of this is transmitted anywhere, except as described above for drive times. It is readable only by the App, and deleting the App deletes all of it. That is the complete data deletion procedure; there is nothing on our side to request the deletion of.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising. The App contains no ads and no advertising SDK.
  • No analytics. There is no analytics or attribution SDK of any kind, from us or anyone else.
  • No tracking. We do not collect the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. No App Tracking Transparency prompt appears because there is nothing to ask you to permit.
  • No crash or usage reporting. Nothing reports back to us when the App is used or fails.
  • No sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share information for advertising. The anonymous purchase record above is used only to unlock Pro.
  • No referral tracking. Opening a course's booking page does not tell us, or earn us anything.
  • No profiling and no automated decisions about you. The App ranks golf courses, not people.

6. Children's Privacy

LinksPulse is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Because the App has no accounts and collects nothing, there is no children's data for us to hold or delete. A parent with questions is welcome to contact us at the address below.

7. Your Rights

Privacy laws including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) give you rights to access, correct, delete and port the personal information a company holds about you, and to object to its processing.

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to produce, correct, export or erase. A Pro purchase record is tied only to a random ID we cannot connect to you; if you want it removed, email us and we will ask you for the details needed to find it. Everything the App keeps is on your own device and under your own control, and uninstalling the App removes it. If you believe we hold information about you, write to us and we will tell you plainly what we find.

8. Security

All network requests the App makes use encrypted HTTPS connections. Because we store nothing, the usual risk of a breach exposing your data does not apply here — there is no store of user data to breach.
